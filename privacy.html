Collab-Sorter — Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 September 2026

Collab-Sorter is a private tool used by one person to triage collaboration offers in their own email inbox. It is not a product, it is not offered to the public, and it has no users other than its owner.

What it accesses

With the account holder's explicit Google consent, Collab-Sorter uses three Gmail permissions: read (gmail.readonly) to find collaboration offers; modify (gmail.modify) to mark a message read or archive it, only when the account holder presses that button; and send (gmail.send) to send a reply, only after the account holder has reviewed the exact text and confirmed it. It never sends, deletes or changes mail on its own.

Where the data goes

Email is fetched to a single personal computer and stored only on that machine. There is no server, no hosting provider, no database, and no account system. Nothing is uploaded to a website.

One third party is involved. To decide whether a message is a genuine collaboration offer or a scam, the text of candidate messages is sent to Anthropic's API to classify them and to draft replies the account holder asks for. Anthropic processes it to return those answers. No other third party receives message content, and the data is not sold, shared, or used for advertising by anyone.

What is kept, and for how long

The tool stores what it needs to rank offers: sender, subject, extracted figures, and its own verdicts. To draft replies, it also keeps the text of likely offers, and the account holder's own replies to those senders, for up to 45 days (at most 20,000 characters each), deleting it sooner once the account holder decides on the offer. Messages identified as scams are never stored. Access tokens are encrypted at rest on the local machine.

Revoking access

The account holder can revoke Collab-Sorter's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking stops all access immediately.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or a request about data: open an issue on GitHub. That is a public tracker, so please do not include anything private in it.
