I build things, then try to prove they don’t work.

The method

  • A test that can’t fail isn’t evidence.
  • A check I can’t re-run is an anecdote.
  • A zero is usually a claim about your query, not about your data.
  • “That’s console-only” usually means nobody has scripted it yet.
  • Complaints tell you what annoys people, not what’s common. Measure the whole population.
  • Every conclusion gets a second reader whose only job is to break it.
  • If someone else can’t reproduce it, it isn’t finished.
